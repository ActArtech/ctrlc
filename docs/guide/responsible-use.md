# Responsible use

CtrlC can rebuild **public page UIs** into React codebases. Use it lawfully and ethically.

## Allowed (examples)

- Migrating a site **you own** off WordPress/Webflow/etc.  
- Recovering a modern codebase when **you have rights** but lost source.  
- Learning layout/animation techniques with permission or public fair use where applicable.  
- Internal redesign sandboxes for your own products.  

## Not allowed

- Phishing, impersonation, or credential capture.  
- Passing off someone else’s brand, logos, or copy as your own product.  
- Bypassing auth, paywalls, or access controls.  
- High-volume scraping that violates a site’s terms of service.  

## Product rules that help

- **React reconstruction**, not “host their HTML and pretend.”  
- **Page-first** scope reduces accidental full-site scraping.  
- **SectionPack** makes reuse explicit (your packs of **your** rebuilt components).
